Our approach

Curated, not collected.

We look at hundreds of pieces for every one that reaches our floor. What stays has real design pedigree, honest materials, and the quiet confidence of something made to last.

Our buyers spend the year at estate sales, private collections, and dealer networks across Europe and North America. Each piece is researched, attributed where possible, and restored by trusted craftspeople. Nothing is over-finished and nothing is passed off as something it isn't. You'll know where it came from, who likely made it, and what's been done to it.

Expertise you can see in every piece.

01

Find

Our buyers work estates, private collections, and a trusted dealer network built over fifteen years.

02

Authenticate
